About Me

Hello!My name is Lenette Magoba, and I am a Diploma student pursuing a course of Information Technology at Uganda Christian University in the Faculty of Engineering, Design and Technology, Department of Computing and Technology. I am a passionate and motivated technology enthusiast who is interested in using technology to solve problems, improve people's lives and create useful digital solutions.

My journey in Information Technology has given me an opportunity to develop both theoretical knowledge and practical skills in different areas of computing. I have developed interests in computer hardware maintenance, programming, computer networking, database systems, Visual Basic and web development . I enjoy learning how different technologies work and putting the knowledge I gain into practice through projects and practical exercises.

Another project I have explored is Water Tuffune, a platform aimed at helping people in communities access clean water more easily. The project focuses on using technologies such as USSD and SMS to connect households with water suppliers, especially in areas where internet access may be limited.

My career goal is to become a skilled Information Technology professional who can design, develop, manage and maintain reliable technology solutions. I would like to continue improving my skills in computer hardware and maintenance,programming software development, networking, databases, cybersecurity and other areas of IT. In the future, I hope to participate in projects that have a positive impact on businesses, communities and society.
